the pigeon

One bird, a few moods. On the site he carries your notes. Here he gets a life of his own, so he can turn up anywhere five days does.

  • the letter

    Straight from leave us a note. The one the kit grew from.

    pigeon-letter-*

  • delivered

    Thank-yous, sent notes, anything that landed.

    pigeon-delivered-*

  • homing

    On its way to someone: invites, reminders, news.

    pigeon-flight-*

  • puffed up

    Announcements, launches, a little swagger.

    pigeon-puff-*

  • the stretch

    Mornings, slow starts, day one of five.

    pigeon-stretch-*

  • two of us

    Matches, couples, a table for two.

    pigeon-pair-*

  • wrong table.

    404s, dead links, anything that went sideways.

    pigeon-lost-*

light

For the grass, the clay, anything dark. Same lines, in the page's cream.

filled

He brings his own paper, a fill and a thin edge, so he sits on photos and busy color without getting lost in them.

Every pose ships in three cuts, ink, light and filled. Each cut is a folder in brand/pigeon/ with png/ and svg/ inside.

To add or change a pose, draw it in brand/pigeons.html as clean paths, then run node brand/render.js.
