privacy notice

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Five Days is a dating experiment run by Kai Douglass and Ruby Lau ("Five Days", "we", "us"). We're the controller responsible for how the information you share with Five Days is used.

If you have any questions about your data, or want to exercise any of the rights below, email datein5days@gmail.com.

what we collect

When you sign up, we may collect information such as your name, age confirmation, contact details, city, Instagram handle or verification photo, dating preferences, answers to our matching questions, availability and feedback.

Some of your dating preferences may reveal sensitive information, including your sexual orientation. Under data protection law, this is a "special category" of data and gets extra protection, described below.

what we use it for, and our legal basis

We only use information for the purposes below, and each one relies on a specific legal basis:

Verify that participants are real
Legitimate interests — keeping the experiment safe and free of fake profiles. The face check itself runs on your device; we only ever see the photo, not a biometric template.
Understand who you might be compatible with, and find potential matches
Performance of a contract, for the matchmaking service you've asked us for. Where your answers reveal sensitive information such as sexual orientation, we rely on your explicit consent instead — see below.
Contact you and arrange dates
Performance of a contract.
Run and improve the Five Days experiment
Legitimate interests, using information in aggregate or de-identified form wherever we can.
Deal with safety or misuse
Legitimate interests, and legal obligation where the law requires it.
Tell you about future Five Days experiments, launches and updates
Consent — only if you've ticked that box. You can withdraw it at any time without affecting anything else.

We use the information needed to provide the matchmaking experience you sign up for. You don't have to give us your information, but we won't be able to match you without the information needed for matching.

sensitive information and explicit consent

Where your answers may reveal sensitive information such as sexual orientation, we use that information only with your explicit consent, collected as a separate, specific checkbox when you RSVP. Ticking the general 18+ confirmation is not enough on its own, and we don't bundle the two together.

You can withdraw this consent at any time by emailing datein5days@gmail.com. If you withdraw it, we'll stop using that information to match you, though we may keep a record that consent was withdrawn.

who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information.

Our website is hosted on Vercel, and we use Google (Sheets and Apps Script) to store and process sign-up information. We may also use other service providers to communicate with participants or process payments, if that becomes part of the experiment.

We may share limited information — generally your first name and the plan — with your potential match where needed to arrange your date.

We don't currently run analytics or tracking tools without a consent mechanism in place. If that changes, we'll list the tool here before it goes live.

international transfers

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK or EEA, including in the United States. Where data-protection law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards, such as the UK and EU Standard Contractual Clauses, before information is transferred.

how long we keep it

  • Verification photos are deleted after verification, and no later than 7 days after they're submitted.
  • Matchmaking information is deleted or anonymised within 3 months after the relevant Five Days cohort, unless we need to keep limited information longer for legal or safety reasons.

your rights

Depending on where you live, you generally have the right to:

  • ask us what information we hold about you, and get a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct information that's wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict or object to certain uses of your information;
  • ask for your information in a portable format; and
  • withdraw any consent you've given us, at any time.

To exercise any of these, email datein5days@gmail.com. We'll respond as quickly as we can, and in any case within the timeframe the law requires.

complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, we'd like the chance to sort it out directly — email us first. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. If you're in the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); if you're elsewhere in the EU, it's the authority in the country where you live or work.

changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the experiment evolves. If we make a material change, we'll update the date at the top and, where appropriate, let participants know directly.
