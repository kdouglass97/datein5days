our story

a hunch, tested by hand.

Five Days started as one sentence we couldn't stop arguing for: matching interests isn't the same as matching lives. So we're testing it the slow way: one city, fifty people, every date planned by the two of us.

that's haven

the mission

Seat two people together for what they want their lives to hold, then plan the evening so all they have to do is show up.

That's the whole job. No feed, no score, no swiping. Two people reading closely, and a table for two somewhere good.

the two of us

Two actual people. Tap a photo.

The best introductions we've seen didn't come from an app. They came from a friend who knew two people well enough to say: you two, Thursday, this place.

That doesn't scale, and we've stopped trying to make it. Fifty people is as many as the two of us can read closely and plan for properly, so fifty is the number. We only open a city where we can be in the room, which is why New York is first.

We read every answer. We pick the person, the place and the time. You get a text, and a table.

Kai & Ruby

the experiment, start to finish

Five parts. Each one is there for a reason.

  1. one

    a sentence

    Matching interests isn't the same as matching lives. We wrote it down before we built anything, and everything here answers to it.

  2. two

    seven questions

    About seven minutes, and not easy ones. The effort is how we know you mean it, and the thoughtful ones get a call.

  3. three

    fifty seats

    As many as two people can match by hand. It's a capacity, not a marketing number.

  4. four

    five days

    From your yes to a table for two, somewhere good. We handle the admin.

  5. five

    one honest answer

    If the matches land, there's something here. If they don't, the hunch was wrong, and we'd rather know in six weeks than in two years.

what we crossed out

The usual parts of a dating product, and what we do instead.

  • A number can't tell you whether someone wants the same Wednesday you do.

  • Two people read every answer. That's the whole pitch.

  • One person, one evening, planned. One good date, not forty okay chats.

  • Free while we test it. You'll see a price before you ever pay one.

  • We look at the profile to check you're real. We won't post, comment, or message from it.

  • We don't. The privacy notice says so in writing.
