questions

10 questions

no.01who picks my match?+

We do, by hand, from your answers. No feed, no swiping.

no.02what does it cost?+

Nothing while we test it. If we keep going, you'll see the price before you ever pay.

no.03do i have to get on a call?+

Not always. We call when your answers make us curious, and it takes 15 minutes.

no.04why do you need my instagram?+

To make sure everyone you meet is a real person. We look at your profile to confirm, and that's all we do with it. No Instagram? A clear photo of your face works too, and only the two of us see it.

no.05what does my match see?+

Your first name and the plan. The rest is for the date.

no.06what if you miss the 5 days?+

Then it didn't work for you, and we'll say so. That's what the experiment is for.

no.07why my number?+

To tell you when your city opens, and to set up the date. Nothing else, and your match never sees it.

no.08which cities?+

New York first. We plan every date ourselves, so we open one city at a time, where we can be there.

no.09what if my city isn't on the list?+

RSVP anyway, and tell us where you are. Those RSVPs decide which city opens next, and whoever asked first goes first.

no.10how do i bring this to my city?+

RSVP, then send it to the people you'd want there too. Every RSVP from a city moves it up the list. If you know the good bars and the people who'd actually show up, help us run one there.
