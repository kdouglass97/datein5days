# For any AI tool working in this repo

Read `CLAUDE.md` and follow it: it is the shipping workflow for every tool, not only Claude.
Then read `context.md` before any change: what the product is, who it's for, the voice (§4),
the design language (§5), and the landmines (§7).

The short version, if you read nothing else:

- The repo is public. Never commit keys, passwords, or anything about a real sign-up.
- Never push to `main`. Work on your own `claude/` branch and open a PR; an Action merges it.
- Copy follows §4 of `context.md`. Visuals answer one question: the printed form, or the hand?
- Haven, the pigeon, is they/them, and is never redrawn: reuse the lines in `note.js`.
